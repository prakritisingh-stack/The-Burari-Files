CASE FILE 001

A morning that changed everything.

On 1 July 2018, eleven members of the Bhatia family were found dead at their home in Sant Nagar, Burari, North Delhi. Reports say ten were found hanging inside the house, and the eldest family member, a woman in her seventies, was found on the floor of another room.

The case drew national attention. This page treats it as a real tragedy involving real people, not as entertainment.

Note: This is an independent student project. It is not an official police archive and it does not show original evidence.

What was written down?

Reports on the case say handwritten notes found in the home became an important part of the investigation.

Written records cannot be read alone. Who wrote them, when, and why all change what they mean, so investigators weigh them alongside other findings.

"A clue is not the same thing as a conclusion."

This site does not copy or invent any quotation from the real notes.

Evidence versus speculation.

1 – Documented

A family tragedy

Eleven members of one family died at their home in Burari on 1 July 2018. Police investigated the deaths and the case was widely reported.

2 – Investigative focus

Notes and family dynamics

Investigators examined handwritten notes and the family's circumstances. Reporting says experts later pointed to a shared set of beliefs within the household as a key part of the explanation.

3 – Not established

Supernatural explanations

The unsettling nature of the case led to speculation. Paranormal claims are not proven facts and are not presented as such here.
